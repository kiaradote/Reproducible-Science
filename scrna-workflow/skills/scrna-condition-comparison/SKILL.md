# scRNA-seq condition comparison (checkpoint 7)

Question answered: what differs between two conditions, in which cell types, and can this dataset support that conclusion at all?
Start only after checkpoints 1 to 6 have no FAIL. Input: the checkpoint 6 object with `obs["cell_type"]`, a condition column, a sample column (donor, patient or animal), raw counts in `layers["counts"]` (without counts the comparison falls back to a linear model on mean log expression and says so), `obsm["X_pca"]`, optionally a batch or library column and `obs["cluster"]`.
Records use the same `{check, status, detail}` format as the other checkpoints. Call `write_cmp_report(records, details, caveats, prefix, question=...)`, show the overall status and do not continue on FAIL without asking the user.
Cells of one sample are not replicates. Every test that decides significance here treats the sample as the unit.

Helpers (loaded from `kernel.py`; need numpy, scipy, pandas, statsmodels, scikit-learn, matplotlib, and pydeseq2 for the negative-binomial model):
`cmp_run`, `cmp_design`, `cmp_abundance`, `cmp_pseudobulk`, `cmp_de_type`, `cmp_null_swaps`, `cmp_consistency`, `cmp_state`, `cmp_enrich`, `cmp_summaries`, `cmp_props`, `cmp_figures`, `cmp_captions`, `cmp_legends`, `write_cmp_report`, `cmp_catalog`, `cmp_matrix`, `cmp_ols`, `cmp_bh`, `cmp_record`, `cmp_named_types`.

## Workflow

1. **State the question first**, in one sentence naming the control and the treatment (for example "what changes in blood cells when interferon-beta is compared with untreated cells from the same donors?"). It is printed at the top of the report.
2. **One call:** `adata, records, details = cmp_run(adata, condition_col, sample_col, type_col="cell_type", batch_col=None, groups=(control, treatment), gene_sets=None, min_units=3, min_cells=10, fdr=0.05, min_lfc=0.5, fig_dir=...)`; then `write_cmp_report(...)`. The checkpoint keeps `uns["comparison"]` and `uns["comparison_de"]` (type, gene, base_mean, log2fc, stat, pvalue, padj); checkpoint 8 ranks genes by `stat` for its pre-ranked enrichment.
3. **Design gate (`cmp_design`).** Replicates: FAIL under 2 samples per condition with at least `min_cells` cells, WARN under `min_units` (3). Pairing: PASS when every sample appears in both conditions (sample then enters the models as a blocking factor). Condition vs batch: WARN when every batch or library holds one condition only, because a treatment effect cannot then be separated from a library effect and pairing by donor does not remove it. Clusters follow condition: WARN when over half of the clusters have 90% or more of their cells from one condition. Cell types follow condition: WARN for any type with 90% or more of its cells in one condition; those names may have moved with the treatment. Testable types need `min_units` samples with `min_cells` cells (in both conditions when paired); the others are listed with the reason and left out of per-type tests. FAIL stops the run before any test.
4. **Abundance (`cmp_abundance`).** Share of each named type per sample. Model 1: quasi-binomial regression on the counts (treatment plus sample when paired; dispersion from the Pearson statistic, never below 1, t-distribution with the residual degrees of freedom). Model 2: regression on centred log-ratio shares. A type counts as changed only when both agree at FDR 0.05. Shares sum to one, so one type growing makes the others shrink; types whose name follows the condition are marked.
5. **Differential expression (`cmp_pseudobulk`, `cmp_de_type`).** Counts of a type's cells are summed per sample and condition; genes need 10 counts in total and non-zero values in at least `min_units` samples. pydeseq2 with `~ sample + condition` (paired) or `~ condition`, run single-process (`DefaultInference(n_cpus=1)`; the default worker pool crashed on Windows). A gene is called when adjusted p is below `fdr` and |log2 fold change| is at least `min_lfc` (0.5, 1.4-fold). If pydeseq2 fails the log-CPM linear model is used and the check warns.
6. **Checks on the differential expression.** Null check: condition labels are swapped within samples (among samples if unpaired and at least 6 samples) 20 times and the log-CPM model must find almost nothing (median at most max(5, 5% of the real count)). Sample consistency: significant genes must keep their direction in a median of at least 75% of the individual pairs.
7. **State change (`cmp_state`).** Logistic regression on the first 30 principal components of the cells of one type, trained without one sample and tested on that sample (unpaired: one control and one treated sample held out). Median held-out AUC of 0.8 or more with a minimum of 0.65 is a clear shift, median 0.65 or more weak. The same test with condition labels shuffled within samples is reported; WARN if it exceeds 0.65.
8. **Pathways (`cmp_enrich`).** Pre-ranked enrichment on the test statistic against the supplied gene sets restricted to the genes measured; only sets below FDR 0.05 are shown.
9. **Figures:** `cmp_abundance.png`, `cmp_de.png`, `cmp_state.png`, and `cmp_pathways.png` when a pathway passes, in `fig_dir` with `figure_captions.json` (one-line captions), `figure_legends.md` (plain-language legends) and the tables `abundance.csv`, `differential_expression.csv`, `state_change.csv`, `pathway_enrichment.csv`.
10. **Read the Not run list.** Transcription-factor activity (needs OmniPath and decoupler), cell-cell communication (needs a ligand-receptor table), spatial association (needs coordinates) and condition-dependent gene modules are not computed; the descriptive version of gene modules is in checkpoint 8.

## Pitfalls

- If each condition is a separate library (the Kang PBMC data: ctrl and stim libraries), every result is a treatment-or-library effect. The checkpoint warns and carries on; do not report the genes as a clean treatment effect.
- In that dataset treatment moved the clusters themselves: CD8 T cells were 16% of control cells and 0.5% of treated cells, NK cells the reverse. The abundance models agreed, but the names, not the cells, are what changed, so the gate marks them and leaves them out of expression tests.
- Cell-level tests (Wilcoxon on single cells) treat thousands of cells as replicates and call thousands of genes; they are not used for significance here.
- A "clear shift" in held-out AUC says that cells look different, not why. With treatment confounded with library it cannot tell the two apart.
- Few samples give wide uncertainty: with 3 per condition only large effects are found. The report states the sample counts.
- The mean-log-expression fallback (no counts) has no count model; its log fold changes are differences of means and the p-values come from a linear model.

## Tests

`test_scrna_compare.py` (17): Benjamini-Hochberg and regression against a reference solution, design passes for a paired replicated experiment, fails without replicates and warns with two, flags condition confounded with library, detects an unpaired design, flags clusters and names that follow the condition, bad inputs fail cleanly, abundance finds planted share changes, abundance invents nothing when shares are equal (regression test for an unscaled dispersion that gave p near 1e-248), pseudobulk sums and drops small units and falls back without counts, differential expression finds planted genes with the right sign only in the right type (negative-binomial and linear model), label-swap null and sample consistency, state change found only in the changed type, pre-ranked enrichment recovers the planted set, a full run writes figures, captions, legends, tables and a question-first report, and the run stops at the design gate without replicates.
