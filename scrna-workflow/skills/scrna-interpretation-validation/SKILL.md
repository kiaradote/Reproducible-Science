# scRNA-seq biological interpretation and validation (checkpoint 8)

Question answered: what do the named cell types and expression programs mean, which of those statements survive changes in analysis choices, and what can this dataset not say?
Start only after checkpoints 1 to 6 have no FAIL. Input: the checkpoint 6 object (`obs["cell_type"]`, `obs["cluster"]`, normalised log `X`, gene names, optionally `layers["counts"]`, `uns["annotation_panel"]`, `uns["dimred"]`, `uns["clustering"]`).
Records use the same `{check, status, detail}` format as the other checkpoints. Call `write_int_report(records, findings, caveats, "interpretation_report")`, show the overall status and do not continue on FAIL without asking the user.
A pathway name describes what a set of marker genes has in common; it is not evidence that the pathway is active. Group comparisons (control vs treatment) belong to checkpoint 7, whose differential-expression table feeds the pre-ranked enrichment here.

Helpers (loaded from `kernel.py`; need scanpy-compatible AnnData, numpy, scipy, pandas, scikit-learn, statsmodels, matplotlib; the sensitivity analysis also uses the clustering and annotation helpers `cl_graph`, `cl_leiden`, `ann_run`, i.e. load `scrna-clustering` and `scrna-cell-type-annotation` first, or keep `scrna_cluster_kernel.py` and `scrna_annotate_kernel.py` importable):
`int_run`, `int_fetch_kegg_gmt`, `int_read_gmt`, `int_background`, `int_filter_sets`, `int_group_genes`, `int_ora`, `int_trim`, `int_bh`, `int_gsea`, `int_score_sets`, `int_nmf`, `int_expectations`, `int_signature_loso`, `int_collect_caveats`, `int_perturb`, `int_findings`, `int_legends`, `int_captions`, `int_figures`, `write_int_report`, `int_catalog`, `int_record`, `int_split`.

## Workflow

1. **Gene sets.** `int_fetch_kegg_gmt(path, "hsa")` downloads KEGG pathways through rest.kegg.jp (run it once and keep the file with the run); `int_read_gmt(path)` reads any GMT file. Reactome could not be downloaded automatically (the site blocks scripted clients), so supply its GMT by hand if wanted. Sets are filtered to 10 to 500 genes among the genes detected in at least 3 cells.
2. **Expectations (optional but recommended).** A CSV of known biology the data must reproduce, including negative controls. Columns: `expectation`, `kind` (`gene_up`, `set_up`, `fraction_range`, `set_enriched`), `column`, `group`, `genes` or `set` (genes separated by `;`), `direction` (`up` default or `down`), `against`, `min_fraction`, `low`, `high`. Write these before looking at the results; expectations chosen after seeing the data test nothing.
3. **Signature (optional).** A dict `{name, genes, label_col, positive, sample_col (or unit_col), by_type}` for a signature written by the analyst (for example an interferon response). It is scored in held-out samples only, see step 6.
4. **One call:** `adata, records, details = int_run(adata, gene_sets=sets, expectations=table, signature=sig, perturb=True, max_cells=5000, caveats=int_collect_caveats(report_dir), fig_dir=...)`, then `write_int_report(records, details["findings"], details["caveats"], prefix)` and save `adata` as the checkpoint (`obsm["X_programs"]`, `uns["program_names"]`, `uns["interpretation"]`).
5. **Pathways.** For each cell type, marker genes come from a fast t-like score against all other cells (top 100 with at least 10% detection and a minimum difference), and over-representation uses a hypergeometric test against the detected genes with Benjamini-Hochberg correction. Redundant pathways (gene overlap above 0.5) are trimmed to the strongest. Pathways that name housekeeping machinery (ribosome, proteasome) and KEGG disease or infection names are flagged in the findings, because they usually reflect generic immune or translation genes. With a ranked differential-expression result (`ranked={group: Series}`), `int_gsea` runs pre-ranked enrichment with a permutation null that depends on set size; without one, "pre-ranked enrichment" stays in the Not run list.
6. **Gene programs.** Non-negative matrix factorisation at several program counts (`nmf_k`), choosing the largest count whose programs are reproducible across random restarts (cosine similarity target 0.8). The report states how many programs are used mostly by one cell type; programs shared by all types are housekeeping or technical.
7. **Held-out samples.** `int_signature_loso`: the signature is scored in each cell type, summed per sample, and each sample in turn is left out; a classifier trained on the other samples must rank the held-out samples' groups correctly (unit-level AUC, permutation p). Samples with fewer than `min_cells` (10) cells are dropped; cell types without at least two samples per group in the remaining data are reported as not testable instead of passed. Cells of one sample are never split between training and test.
8. **Sensitivity analysis.** `int_perturb` repeats variable genes, PCA, graph, Leiden clustering and annotation on a subsample of at most `max_cells` cells under ten changes (two seeds, two 80% subsamples, 1000 and 3000 variable genes, 3 fewer and 5 more PCs, resolution x0.6 and x1.5; override with `perturb_specs`). Clusters are robust when the median adjusted Rand index is 0.8 or more. A cell-type name is robust when its cells keep that name with median 0.9 and minimum 0.75, sensitive when median 0.7 or more, otherwise unstable. Pathway sets are robust when the median Jaccard overlap with the baseline is 0.6 or more. A failed variable-gene fit falls back to ranking genes by variance.
9. **Findings and caveats.** Every finding (a cell type is present, a pathway marks it, a program belongs to it, an expectation is met) carries its evidence, a robustness label and the caveats that apply. Warnings and unrun checks from the earlier checkpoints' reports are listed in the report and attached to the conclusions (`int_collect_caveats` skips this checkpoint's own previous report).
10. **Figures:** `int_pathways.png`, `int_programs.png`, `int_robustness.png`, `int_validation.png` in `fig_dir` with `figure_captions.json` (one-line captions), `figure_legends.md` (plain-language legends), `findings.csv` and the underlying tables.
11. **Read the Not run list.** Pre-ranked enrichment waits for checkpoint 7; expectations and signature validation are Not run when no file or signature is given.

## Pitfalls

- Over-representation on 100 marker genes finds the biggest, most generic gene sets first: Ribosome led CD4 T cells and B cells in the Kang PBMC test. Read the robustness label and the flag before the name.
- An expectation that fails is a finding, not a bug in the table: platelet genes (PPBP, PF4) leaked into T-cell clusters in the Kang data, which is also why the megakaryocyte-labelled cluster was unstable.
- A perfect held-out AUC with few samples proves little: with 8 donors per group, six cell types reached AUC 1.00 but the permutation p-value was still at the 0.03 limit.
- Treatment can drive clustering: in the Kang data clusters were almost pure by condition, so NK and CD8 T names moved with condition (NK unstable, median 0%). A cell type that follows the treatment cannot be compared across conditions until it is fixed by a reference or by integration.
- Sensitivity runs use a subsample and a simplified annotation, so they show how much the conclusions depend on choices, not what the full-data answer would be.
- The KEGG fetch needs internet access; for offline use supply a GMT file.
